CIPHER NOIR

Case Files of a Private Detective
15 case files · 15 suspects each · 3 decoding disciplines · turn the vault to find the truth

How To Play

1. Read the Brief

Every new investigation pulls a random case from the archive — a new victim, a new setting, a new body found somewhere on the grounds. Read the brief to learn where it happened.

2. Search the Locations

Search every location for evidence. Some hold a single clue, others hide up to five.

3. Crack the Vault

Every clue is sealed behind one of three locks: a Caesar letter-shift, a Hexadecimal byte cipher, or a corrupted Morse signal. Drag the vault dial (or use the slider) until the plaintext panel resolves, then press Lock In Decryption.

4. Interrogate the Suspects

Up to fifteen people were present, each with a distinct face, temperament, and tell. Ask each their questions — watch how they react.

5. Keep a Journal

Every search, every decoded clue, and every conversation is logged automatically, with the suspect's own portrait beside it. Add your own notes too.

6. File Your Accusation

When you're certain, open the Case Board and accuse one or two suspects. One filing per case.

7. Rise in Rank

Solved cases raise your detective rank. Wrong accusations count against your record but never stop you investigating again.
